27. The Final Project in One Sentence
If someone asks you "What exactly did you build?", the answer should be:
ShelfMind is an RL-based autonomous retail inventory and shelf management system that uses an MDP and Value Iteration as its core decision framework, compares it with Q-learning and Deep Q-Networks under dynamic demand and competitive shelf constraints, integrates computer-vision observations from ShelfWatch, and supports explainable human-approved or automatic inventory decisions.
That is a very defensible project statement.
28. And the Research Question
I would frame the technical investigation around:
Can reinforcement learning dynamically optimize inventory replenishment and shelf allocation under changing demand, limited shelf capacity and competing product demand, while outperforming conventional inventory policies?
Then your experiments naturally become:
